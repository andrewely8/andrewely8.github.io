This is a software engineering and AI research project I am currently developing around prediction markets. The application collects and replays historical Kalshi order-book data, allowing users to test algorithmic trading strategies against a simulated exchange environment. I am also experimenting with modern AI workflows, including locally hosted language models and agentic research tools that can inspect market data, generate strategy ideas, and evaluate results. The project is still under active development as I continue expanding the simulator, backtesting system, and AI-assisted research features.
